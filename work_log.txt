2026-06-27 — explored porting Metamon/AMAGO (the tcgmon package) — ABANDONED; kept the simple starter.
- Tried reproducing Metamon's IL-warmup->RL recipe on the AMAGO engine via a new tcgmon/ package
  (BC-distill the rule-based Lucario teacher into a model-free pointer policy, then offline RL).
  Outcome: BC distillation worked (~82% teacher-agreement, beats random ~70-75%, ~parity vs the
  teacher) but NO offline-RL/self-improvement variant (binary/shaped/iterated/self-imitation/DAgger)
  beat BC — the bottleneck is the noisy sparse reward + the teacher's lookahead (cg Search API), which
  a feedforward policy can't represent. Search-at-inference (flat-MC, then a real PUCT tree) lifted it
  to ~parity with the teacher (~48%) but not reliably past it. Reproduces eval-training-not-helping-yet.
- The SIMPLE starter MATCHES the AMAGO port (so AMAGO/Metamon weren't worth the complexity): a
  cross-entropy BC of the teacher into tcg_model.MyModel matches AMAGO's prior (greedy 40% vs teacher
  at n=200, = AMAGO flat-MC) and rollout-MCTS @ search_count>=30 reaches ~45% (= AMAGO's 48% within
  noise). AMAGO's recurrence buys nothing here.
- DECISION: dropped the port. Removed amago/, metamon/, tcgmon/, tcgmon_plan.md and the out/tcgmon_*
  checkpoints. KEEPERS (all pure-starter, no external RL lib):
    * train_mymodel_bc.py --policy-loss ce  -> out/mymodel_lucario_bc_ce.pth (BC-distill the teacher
      into MyModel; the native Huber-on-±1 policy loss was the weak imitator, CE fixed it 59%->85.7%).
    * eval_mymodel.py — seat-swapped Wilson-CI eval of the BC MyModel vs random/teacher.
    * tcg_model.mcts_agent value_mode="rollout" (default "net" unchanged) — rollout leaf eval.
    * starter improvements kept: starter_rl_kaggle.py --deck (route the learner deck) + the
      mirror-determinization fix in mcts_agent; eval.py --checkpoints round-robin mode.
- What does NOT help (verified, don't retry): self-play training (cold AND warm-start from BC ->
  DIVERGES vs the teacher); the MyModel value head (miscalibrated on the ~50/50 mirror -> search hurts).
  EVAL DISCIPLINE: vs-teacher win-rate has high game-to-game variance — trust n>=200 (n=60 swung 49 vs
  the reliable 40).
